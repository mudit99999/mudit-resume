About

Profile

Entry-level cybersecurity professional based in Nagpur, India, with internship experience in vulnerability assessment and web application security testing, plus hands-on exposure to live cybercrime casework.

At the Cyber Cell Police Station I assisted on live cyber fraud, online scam and social media abuse cases — supporting FIR documentation, digital evidence collection and case records, and helping victims get their reports moving. That work taught me to read suspicious activity carefully and treat evidence as something that has to hold up later.

At Phoenix Cyber Secure I tested web applications, learned bug hunting fundamentals and identified straightforward security issues. Earlier, at Crypto Forensic Technology, I built the groundwork: ethical hacking fundamentals, reconnaissance, scanning and enumeration.

Alongside the technical work I'm improving my verbal communication and presentation skills through continuous practice and technical discussions.

Practical cybersecurity experience with an investigative mindset.
